Services

Stills, film and drone from one shoot

Book any one on its own, or combine them so your listing photos, walkthrough and aerials are shot on the same day, in the same light.

Photography

Edited, listing-ready stills of every room, the outside spaces and the details that sell the home.

  • Interiors and exteriors
  • Detail and lifestyle frames
  • Colour-corrected, verticals straightened

Video package

One package from first conversation to final cut. We agree on what the video needs to do for the listing, then I film the property on the ground and from the air and edit it to that brief.

  • Planning call on style, length and where it will run
  • Filming on the ground and by drone
  • Edited, colour graded and set to music
  • Versions for portals, YouTube and Instagram

Drone

Aerial stills and footage that show the plot, the roofline, the garden and what's around the property.

  • Aerial stills
  • Aerial video, included in the video package
  • Location and views in context

Most listings book all three together, so stills, film and aerials are done in one visit.
